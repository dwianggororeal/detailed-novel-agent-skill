# World Rules Evaluation & Logical Robustness Rubric

This document evaluates the logical soundness, economic plausibility, and mechanical balance of **World Rules** before story execution.

---

## 1. World Logic Evaluation Rubric (Scale 1 - 5)

| Dimension | Weight | Description | Minimum Passing Standard (4/5) |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Energy System & Cost Balance** | 30% | Magic/energy has tangible physical tolls and limitations (*no free power*). | Clear bodily fatigue limits and severe overuse backlash. |
| **2. Sociological Logic & Racial Friction** | 25% | Tensions and prejudices stem from plausible historical roots with regional nuances. | Realistic social dynamics avoiding flat black-and-white caricatures. |
| **3. Guild Economics & Labor Realism** | 25% | Labor market oversupply, quest pricing, and class exam bureaucracies make economic sense. | Market forces and regulations create natural stakes for adventurers. |
| **4. Monster Ecology & Threat Hierarchy** | 20% | Monsters possess distinct population tiers, resource utilities, and threat levels. | Monster materials align realistically with societal needs and crafting. |

## 3. World Logic Stress Tests

1. **The Labor Oversupply Test:** If adventurer supply spikes in a city, do quest rewards realistically decrease and guild competition intensify?
2. **The Mana Battery Exploitation Test:** Is there any loophole where mana stones can be cycled infinitely without requiring human bodily recovery periods?
3. **The Racial Stereotype Nuance Test:** Do racial dynamics vary realistically across territories (e.g. tyrannical regions vs integrated open trading hubs)?
