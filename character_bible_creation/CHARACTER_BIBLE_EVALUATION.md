# Character Bible Evaluation & Robustness Rubric

This document evaluates characterization depth, reader memorability (*branding*), and alignment with the Lore Bible.

---

## 1. Characterization Evaluation Rubric (Scale 1 - 5)

| Dimension | Weight | Description | Minimum Passing Standard (4/5) |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Branding & Memorability** | 30% | Character features a distinct visual silhouette, micro-habits, and authentic, non-cringe dialogue. | Recognizable within a single scene without repetitive name tagging. |
| **2. Psychological Depth & Motivation** | 25% | Motivations possess emotional roots, clear trauma triggers, and a solid moral compass. | Actions are driven by rational, dynamic internal needs. |
| **3. Lore Bible Synchronization** | 25% | Race, abilities, origins, and combat capabilities adhere 100% to established world mechanics. | Zero violations of magic system limits and world laws. |
| **4. Action & Dialogue Consistency** | 20% | Spoken lines feel natural and physical actions strictly reflect internal ethics. | Zero soap-opera melodrama; grounded dialogue delivery. |

## 3. Characterization Stress Tests

1. **The Moral Dilemma Test:** When forced to choose between their *Primary Goal* and their *Absolute Red Line*, does their decision remain logical and consistent with past trauma?
2. **The Silhouette Test:** If character names and visual descriptions are obscured, can a reader still identify the speaker purely by dialogue cadence and physical micro-gestures?
3. **The Power Limit Test:** Under mortal danger, does the character remain bound by magic limits without manifesting unearned plot armor?
