# Prose Narration Evaluation & Diction Rubric

This document evaluates the readability, plain diction, and action-dialogue fluidity of final novel prose paragraphs.

---

## 1. Prose Narration Evaluation Rubric (Scale 1 - 5)

| Dimension | Weight | Description | Minimum Passing Standard (4/5) |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Plain Diction & Common Verbs** | 30% | Uses natural, accessible everyday verbs free of obscure literary jargon. | 0 archaic verbs; effortless reading comprehension. |
| **2. Zero Purple Prose & Sugar-Coating** | 25% | Completely free from abstract metaphors and flowery overwriting. | 100% grounded in tangible physical reality. |
| **3. Fluid Merger & Action-Dialogue Blend** | 25% | Dialogues, delivery tags, and physical actions blend organically. | Paragraph breaks and conversational pauses feel natural. |
| **4. Preservation of Slowburn Pacing** | 20% | Micro-gestures from the loglines are preserved without rushed cutting. | Scene tempo remains deep and atmospheric. |

## 3. Archaic Word & Purple Prose Scanner

Before approving final prose, ensure **NONE** of the following patterns appear:
- ❌ **Archaic / Obscure Verbs:** Overly literary words that obscure meaning.
- ❌ **Abstract Metaphors:** "weaving the loom of silence", "song of the wind", "groping the walls of destiny".
- ✅ **Clean, Concrete Verbs:** *opened, glanced, stroked, snorted, stepped, pulled, held, turned, stood*.
